    You've earned this money three times over.
    So where is it?

    Salary enters on the 25th. For the first three days you feel rich. Then the transfers start. Then the debit alerts. Then by the 18th you are scrolling your bank app at 11pm, muttering "this can't be right."

    Most salary earners in Nigeria can't tell you where 30-40% of their salary went. Not because they're careless. Because the money leaves in pieces too small to notice — until you add them up on paper.

    • ₦50 stamp duty on every transfer — you did 47 of them last month.
    • "Borrow me 2k" messages that never came back — 6 people, 12k.
    • Data, airtime, Netflix, Spotify, gym — subscriptions you forgot you had.
    • Loan-app debits from that one week in July — still paying for it.
    • Fuel + generator during the three weeks NEPA took.
    • The one weekend — aso ebi, owambe, December, school fees, a January you didn't plan for.

    The Autopsy doesn't judge you. It lays the body on the table. You sign the verdict.
