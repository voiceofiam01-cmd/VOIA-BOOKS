Drill8 minutes every payday, before you spend
  1. Diagnose (10 minutes). Fixed costs ÷ income = your number. Over 70%? The structure is the problem — not your shawarma.
  2. Plug the 3 leaks. Delivery fees, sleeping subscriptions, the 8pm–11pm scroll-and-buy window. Track leaks, not kobo.
  3. Split every payday (8 minutes). Bills (60–70%), Life (20–25%), Buffer (5–10%), Future You (5%). The drill runs before you spend one naira.
  4. Spend smarter, not less. Swap instead of cancel. Anchor the market run. Say no with a yes.
  5. Defend the escape. Forecast Wall for December, rent, school fees. Sinking funds so nothing ambushes you.
  6. When a month catches fire: the triage card — stop the bleeding, map the minefield, find ₦20,000, bridge honestly.

Is this another “stop buying shawarma” budget book?

No. Chapter 1 proves that shawarma is not the problem — fixed costs and invisible leaks are. There is no suffering, no guilt, no “track every kobo” exercise.

What format do I get, and how is it delivered?

You get a 66-page PDF that opens on any phone. Right after you pay, you land on a page with a big button that opens the book instantly in Google Drive — no waiting for email, no login required. Tap the download icon inside Drive to save it to your phone and read offline any time.

Will this work if I earn less than ₦300k — or more than ₦1M?

Yes. The plan is built around your number — fixed costs ÷ income — not your salary size. If you earn ₦180k or ₦1.8M, the same four-word framework applies; only the numbers change. The irregular-income chapter covers freelancers, drivers and business owners too.

Do I need a second job or side hustle?

No. The core plan doesn't ask you to earn more. It fixes what's already coming in. There is an appendix called The No-Second-Job Income Menu for when the honest maths says you do need more — five hours a week, firewall intact.

Section 7 walks you through the debt spiral exit: the debt inventory, snowball vs avalanche, the autopay floor, and how to ask for a restructure. The Triage Protocol also covers a bridge plan when this month is already on fire.
