About the house

A small publishing house for ordinary truth.

Voice of I AM is an independent imprint, founded in 2026 and based in Nigeria. We publish two collections of books: short, plain-English guides for the problems you live with, and longer, slower writing for the questions that live underneath.

Why this house existsMost books you can buy were not written for your life.

You are a salary earner staring at your account on the 18th. You are a daughter supporting a family at 28. You are a person watching your body change in your fifties. You are a worker on a Sunday evening, wondering how five more years of this can pass. You are someone carrying a question no one around you seems to be asking.

The big publishing houses in New York and London don't write for your context. The loud online coaches don't write in your accent. The people close to you love you, but they don't always have language for what you are feeling.

We try to write those books — the one you'd hand to a friend, with their actual struggle, in their actual language, under the actual conditions of this life. Sometimes short and direct. Sometimes long and slow. Always honest.

"We are not trying to sell you a new life. We are trying to help you meet the one you already have." — From the house editorial note

We are small. We are independent. We are funded by readers — not investors, not ad deals, not a big publishing parent company. That freedom matters. It means we can write what is true even when it doesn't scale, even when it doesn't fit a marketing category, even when it takes longer than a quarter to finish.

Two collectionsOne house. Two registers.

Some days you need a clear answer before Monday. Some nights you need company in a question that won't resolve. We publish both, and we don't confuse the two.

I · The Real You Library

Short books. Real problems. One shift at a time.

Direct, compact books by Elise M.N. — each focused on one problem, one question, or one meaningful shift. 60–110 pages. Written the way you would actually explain it to a friend.

These are the books you can start and finish the same evening the problem shows up. No filler. No homework. No jargon. Just something you can use on Tuesday.

  • Money, salary cycles, debt, family support
  • Relationships, repair, conversations
  • Wellness, aging, rest, care
  • Career, work, quiet ambition
  • The patterns you keep repeating
II · The Longform Collection

Deeper books for the questions underneath.

Longer, slower works — philosophy, memoir, spiritual reflection, fiction. Not advice. Not answers on a deadline. Books to sit with, return to, and let work on you over time.

This collection grows more slowly, on purpose. Some of these manuscripts have been in progress for years. They will be released when they are ready, not before.

  • The inner life
  • Philosophy and spiritual depth
  • Memoir and reflection
  • Literary fiction
  • The questions without answers

Why two collections, not one?

Because "read this 80-page book tonight and fix your budget by Friday" is a different promise than "read this over a winter and let it rearrange you slowly." They serve different moments in a life. We didn't want to dress one up as the other.

Author · The Real You Library

Elise writes the short, direct books that anchor The Real You Library. The work starts from one conviction: most of what people need is not more information, but clearer ways of seeing what is already in front of them.

Rather than offering complicated systems or one-size-fits-all answers, Elise writes in small, plain sentences about the recurring problems of ordinary life — the ones that show up in DMs, in kitchen conversations, in voice notes at 11pm.

Each book takes one subject at a time: one problem, one question, one shift. No motivational yelling. No side-door sermons. Just the thing, said as cleanly as it can be said.

"I am not trying to be the voice of anything grand. I'm just trying to write the book I wish someone had handed me, on the day I first needed it."

On the Longform Collection

The Longform Collection will include writing by Elise and by other voices whose work fits the house. Those names will be announced as each book approaches publication. We don't list authors before their manuscripts are ready — it tends to make everyone write worse.

Editorial commitmentsWhat we keep coming back to.

Clarity over cleverness.

If a sentence doesn't help you see something more clearly, we cut it. Smaller words. Same truth.

Respect over motivation.

We don't yell at you to "hustle harder" or "level up." Most of life is not a level. We write like we're sitting across from you.

Methods over miracles.

We're interested in things that work on a Tuesday, in real life, under real conditions — not in a perfect version of you.

Truth over trends.

We won't write to a viral sound. We write to the thing that's still true three years from now.

Patience over productivity.

The short books are short on purpose. The long books are long on purpose. We don't rush either.

Honesty over authority.

We don't pretend to have answers we don't have. When a question is too large for a fix, we say so.

And a few things we refusePublishing has habits we are not inheriting.

  • 01

    No fake urgency.

    No countdown timers. No "only 3 copies left." No invented deadlines. Buy the book when you need the book. The book will be there.

  • 02

    No invented readers.

    We don't write fake reviews. If you see a quote, it is from a real person who gave permission to share it.

  • 03

    No guarantees.

    A book is a book. We don't promise you'll get rich, calm, married, or healed. We promise the book is honest.

  • 04

    No bait-and-switch.

    Buy the book. Read the book. There is no $500 course in the appendix. There is no funnel.

  • 05

    No filler.

    A short book is not a failed long book. We write until the idea is done, then stop.

  • 06

    No shame.

    We don't write at readers. We write from somewhere close to the problem. You are not the first person to be here.
