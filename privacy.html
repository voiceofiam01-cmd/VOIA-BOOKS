1. Who we are

Voice of I AM is an independent publishing house based in Nigeria. We publish The Real You Library (short, direct books by Elise M.N.) and the Longform Collection. This privacy policy covers all pages under the voiceofiam01-cmd.github.io/VOIA-BOOKS/ domain and any of our book landing pages hosted on it.

2. What information we collect

Information you give us directly

  • At checkout: your email address and full name, as entered on Paystack. These are required to process your payment and send your receipt.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp, TikTok DM, or by any other support channel.
  • Feedback: reviews, testimonials, or early-reader comments you voluntarily send us (we only share these publicly with your explicit permission).

Payment information

All payments are processed by Paystack. We never see, store, or handle your card number, BVN, bank login, OTP, or PIN. Paystack's own privacy policy governs how they handle that information — see Section 5.

Information collected automatically

  • Meta Pixel: when you visit our pages, the Meta (Facebook) Pixel records aggregate, anonymised events such as page views, button clicks, and purchases, using cookies and similar technologies. This helps us understand which pages and ads are useful. It does not identify you to us personally unless you are logged into Facebook, and even then the data is aggregated.
  • Log data: GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, automatically collects standard server logs (IP address, browser type, referring page, timestamps). This is normal for any website and is used only in aggregate to diagnose problems.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect only for these purposes:

  • To process payments and deliver the books you buy.
  • To send you a payment receipt and give you access to your download.
  • To reply to your support messages and resolve problems (e.g., a Drive link not opening).
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people find our books and which pages are useful, so we can improve the site and write better books.
  • Very occasionally, to tell you about a new book we've published — but only if you've explicitly opted in to hear from us. You can opt out at any time.

4. What we never do

These are our hard rules:
  • We never sell, rent, or share your email address, phone number, or personal information with any third party for their own marketing.
  • We never ask you for your bank login, BVN, OTP, card PIN, or card CVV outside of Paystack's official checkout page. Any message claiming to be us and asking for these is fake — report it immediately.
  • We never store credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment instruments on our own servers.
  • We never use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling beyond standard ad-platform measurement.

5. Third-party services

We use the following services to run the website and sell books. Each has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read:

  • Paystack — payment processing for all book sales in Naira. Privacy policy: paystack.com/terms
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. Privacy statement: GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Drive — hosts the downloadable ebook PDFs after purchase. Privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) — provides the Meta Pixel for anonymous traffic measurement and powers our ads. Privacy policy: Meta Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp — our primary support channel. Privacy policy: WhatsApp Privacy Policy
  • TikTok — where we post book content and may run ads. Privacy policy: TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — serves the fonts on this website. Privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy

6. Cookies and tracking

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website. We use cookies only through the Meta Pixel to help us measure traffic and ad performance in aggregate.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies won't prevent you from buying or reading our books, but it may stop us from seeing that you visited — which is completely fine with us.

Where we run ads, Meta, TikTok and other platforms may use their own cookies to show our ads to people likely to be interested. This is governed by those platforms' privacy settings; you can opt out of ad tracking in your account settings on each platform.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Purchase records: Paystack keeps transaction records for as long as required by Nigerian financial regulations (typically 6 years for tax and audit purposes). We can see your name, email, the book you bought, and the amount — but not your payment details.
  • WhatsApp messages: kept in our chat thread as long as needed to support you, then deleted on request.
  • Aggregate analytics: anonymised page-view data has no personal identifier and may be kept indefinitely to understand long-term trends.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of these rights:

  • Access: ask us what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your personal information (subject to records we must keep for legal/tax reasons, e.g., payment records required by law).
  • Objection: ask us to stop processing your data in certain ways (e.g., marketing messages).
  • Data portability: request a copy of the data you provided in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp (see Section 12). We will respond within 7 days.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information:

  • All payments are handled by Paystack, which is PCI-DSS compliant and uses industry-standard encryption.
  • This site is served over HTTPS only.
  • We don't collect more data than we need.
  • We never ask for sensitive credentials (OTP, BVN, card PIN) by message, email, or phone.

No online service is 100% secure, so please use a unique password for your Paystack/email accounts and message us immediately if you notice anything suspicious.

10. Children

Our books and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA). If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally — for example, if we add a new service or the law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version. Material changes will be noted on our home page or by message to readers who have opted in.
