The loop you already run

Every anxious spiral runs the same five stops.

Once you can see the track, you can get off at stage two instead of stage five.

1 · TriggerA slow reply. "I'm tired today," said flat. Your brain whispers: they're leaving.
2 · ObsessionYou check last-seen, re-read chats, can't focus on work.
3 · DesperationYou double-text. Overly nice. Your own plans abandoned.
4 · MeltdownShort reply or none. Chest physically hurts. I knew it. I'm too much.
5 · ShameNext morning you hate yourself, promise detachment. Until the next trigger.

The stage name is the exit door. You don't will your way off this loop. You name the stop you're at.

A triggered emotion peaks and starts clearing in about ninety seconds — if you stop re-feeding it with story. What keeps a spiral alive for three hours isn't the trigger. It's the forty rounds of re-reading you poured on it. — Section 1, The 90-Second Wave

Questions

The ones you're already asking.

Is this the same book as Stop Overthinking Your Relationship?

No. That 14-day program is the interrupt protocol — naming the loop, the 90-second urge observation, the basics. The Detox is the deeper nervous-system reset for people whose pattern runs further back than texting: anxious attachment, protest behavior, the chase/withdraw loop with avoidants, jealousy, dating, the maintenance year, the body toolkit. If you did the 14-day program and the spiral still owns your chest at 2am, this is next.

Will this turn me cold or detached?

No. The goal is not to care less. The goal is to stop needing the other person to regulate your nervous system every five minutes. People who finish this usually love more clearly, not less.

Is this therapy?

No. It is a structured educational program. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. It pairs well with a good therapist. If you are in crisis, reach help first.

What if the person I'm with is actually avoidant?

Section 11 is written explicitly for this dynamic. It does not tell you to chase harder or to detach permanently as a flex. It gives you the loop and the only exit that works — which is neither.

How long is it?

Roughly 10,000 words — about 95 typeset pages, plus appendices. The 30-day plan gives you one move a day for the first month. The second month plan takes you from brake-owner to driver.
