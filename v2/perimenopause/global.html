Questions

The ones you're asking.

Is this HRT propaganda?

No. One lane out of five is the hormone lane. The others are local estrogen, non-hormonal prescriptions, therapy, and lifestyle. The book translates every option honestly, including risks and contraindications. You make the decision with your doctor, armed with better questions.

I'm 38. Too early?

Perimenopause can start in your late thirties. The swing and constellation of symptoms is the signal, not your age. If the list matches, the decoder is useful regardless of the birthday.

Is this book only for married women / mothers / Nigerians?

No. The symptoms, the doctor's-office problem, the treatment menu, and the sleep chapter are universal. The scripts and the hot-flash card were written for Nigerian life first, but they travel.

Will this cure me?

No. There is no cure for a transition. There is a map, a set of tools, better questions, and a way to walk into your next appointment with paper instead of panic. That is what is being sold here.

How long is it?

Roughly 10,000 words — about 100 typeset pages plus printable field kits. Short enough to read between appointments. Dense enough to re-read.
