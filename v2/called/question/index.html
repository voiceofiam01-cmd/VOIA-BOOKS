The question arrived
before you knew
you were asking.

It is 3am. The house is quiet. You are not sad. You are not angry. You are suddenly, sharply, awake to a single sentence you have been avoiding for years.

What am I doing — and who asked me to?

Ten pages. Free. The opening of the book people are already arguing about.

The definition, in three sentences A call arrives.
Consent turns it into a commission.
The commission is a body of work that must outlive you.

Everything else is those three sentences, unfolded.

What this read does

It does not give you an experience.
It names what is already happening.

NamesThe difference between the call arriving and the awakening itself — the line almost every teacher on the subject deliberately blurs.
CorrectsThe lie that spiritual awakening is "not for the weak." It is for the weak. Weakness is the only available starting point any of us have.
WarnsThat refusing a call does not switch it off — it reroutes it. Into the body. Into the drinking. Into the rage with no proportion. And tells you what to do instead.

Includes Appendix A — one page for the person in crisis tonight.
Wanting to die is not a stage of awakening. It is an emergency, and the book says so plainly.
