Is Somebody Calling You?
A short essay. PDF. A few minutes to read.
Open the essay →

Read it once. Then sit with it for a day. This is not a quiz to pass.

The question is not the answer. The essay does not hand you a calling. It hands you a clean way to ask the question — so you stop borrowing other people's answers for a question that was always yours. Read it slowly. Answer the prompts in the margin in pencil. Let it sit. Callings announce slowly.

How to use it

  1. Print it if you can. Questions answered on paper look different than questions answered on a screen.
  2. Do not answer everything tonight. Just read. Underline the sentence that landed hardest. That is enough for tonight.
  3. Come back to it in the morning. The answer is never the one you reach for at 11pm.
If the question has weight

The question is the opening. The book is the rest of the room.

If the question is still sitting with you after three days, Somebody Called You is the full book — how you hear it clearly, how you stop hiding in the small life, how you answer without burning your real life down in a weekend, and what obedience actually looks like when you have a rent to pay and people who depend on you.

If the call has roots, Ancestor You Are Becoming is the companion — the seven movements from seed to shade.
