How we publish

A few things we refuse to do.

Publishing has a set of habits we are not interested in inheriting. These are the promises a reader can hold us to.

— 01

No fake urgency.

No countdown timers, no "only 4 copies left," no manufactured scarcity. Buy when you need the book. The book will still be there.

— 02

No fake readers.

We don't invent reviews. If you see a reader quote, it's from a real person who gave permission to share it.

— 03

No hidden funnels.

Buy the book. Read the book. There is no $500 course in the appendix. No upsell on the thank-you page. The book is the product.

— 04

No shame.

We don't write at readers. We write from somewhere close to the problem. You are not the first person to be here.

— 05

No guarantees.

We don't promise the book will make you rich, married, healed, or calm. It's a book. Results are your own.

— 06

No filler.

A short book is not a failed long book. We write until the idea is done, then stop. Real-You books stay short on purpose.

After the noise

A book is the quietest thing you can buy.

No algorithm inside. No notifications. No notifications inside the notifications. Just a voice, a page, and the small hour after everyone has gone to sleep.

We make books worth taking into that hour. Pick one that names the thing you have been carrying.
