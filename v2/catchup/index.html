The tool you actually use

Forget eight times salary.
Use the 300 multiplier.

The number you actually need hangs off one thing: what you will spend. Not what a brochure says you should have saved. Take the monthly amount your savings must cover in retirement. Subtract whatever pension, government benefit, or rental income will cover. Multiply the gap by 300. That is roughly the pot you need.

Fog becomes a number. Numbers can be worked.

The 24-hour panic protocol

For the day after you open the statement.

  • Hour 0–1: Close the tabs. No retirement articles. No 8×-salary calculators. No late-night finance videos selling certainty.
  • Hours 1–24: Write essentials. Write benefit estimate. Multiply gap by 300. Tell one trusted human. That is the whole day's work.
  • Banned for one week: no cashing out (taxes and penalties take a third before breakfast), no pausing contributions "to think," no moving everything to cash at the bad moment, no buying anything that promises to fix it fast.

Reread-worthy rule: the number is information, not an instruction. The instruction arrives in week two, calm, wearing a plan.
